Sound Audition

Story 10.13 · engine retune

Every sound the game ships, synthesized live in your browser by the game's own audio code. No sound files. Click START SOUND first: browsers need a click before they will make any sound.

If this bar moves but you hear nothing, the tab or the computer is muted. TEST TONE plays the simplest possible sound (a plain beep) straight to your speakers, skipping everything else on this page.

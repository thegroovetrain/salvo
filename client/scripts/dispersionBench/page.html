Dispersion Bench

Story 11.1 — real dispersion, the numbers before the game

Where a battleship shell lands around the point you aimed at. Every landing on this page comes from the game's own shared dispersion code — the same function the server will fire with — run live in your browser with the dials below. Nothing here is wired into the game yet.

The landing area is an oval centered on the aim point, its long side along the line of fire. Shells bunch toward the middle and never land outside the oval.

The dials

Shells per salvo

"How tightly bunched": the oval's edge sits this many standard deviations out on a bell curve. Higher puts more shells near the middle; at 0.5 the spread is nearly even across the oval.

The pattern, to scale

Top-down around the aim point (the cross), shells coming in from the left. Solid oval: where a shell can land. Dashed oval: the middle half (half the length, half the width). Each landing shows its u blast circle — bright when the blast reaches the hull, muted when it misses. Drag the hull to move it.

Aim marker ideas

Each idea draws one filled oval per shell of the next turret to fire: where that shell can land, at the range set above. No lines, no blast circles. Long side along the line of fire (shells come in from the left).

The water behind them copies the game's battle view in its own colors. Pick which kind of water the aim point sits on.

Every shell of a salvo aims at the one point, so the ovals stack into one.

Color for ideas D to H

Copied from the game exactly: the ocean, fog and sight hole, range ring, island colors, radar paint colors and strength, storm, star-shell glow and the reticle.

Approximated: the island shape (the real one needs a whole generated map), the radar return's spread (one cell of blue and one of green around a real hull outline on the real 9 u grid), the fog bank's puffs (fixed, not the game's drifting noise), and the radar sweep line is left out.

The backdrop is a stage set, not where the shooter really is. At 1,000 u the aim point is outside your 500 u sight, so in the game it usually sits on fog.

Story 11.3 — the arc: a far radar contact is sharp in range and wide in bearing

A real radar beam has a width, so a far ship does not paint as its own shape: it paints as an arc, wide across the line from you to it and thin along it. Each lit cell of a ship's sharp radar paint is spread sideways by the beam width (centered on the ship's true direction from you) and along the line of sight by a short pulse. Then today's fuzz runs on top, unchanged.

The map, the hull shapes, the arc and the fuzz on this page are the game's own shared code, run live in your browser. No island shadows are drawn here: this page shows only the arc. Nothing here is wired into the game yet.

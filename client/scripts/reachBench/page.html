Story 11.2 — the hectometer ruler and real reach, the numbers before the game

Every distance in the game on one ruler: hectometers (hm). One hectometer is 100 meters; the 10,000 u board is a real 44 km sea, so 1 hm ≈ 22.7 u. The map, the hull shapes, the shadow math and the speeds on this page are the game's own shared code, run live in your browser. Nothing here is wired into the game yet.

Set the numbers with the sliders, pick a look in "The display", then press "Copy numbers" at the bottom and paste the result back.

1 · The board

A real map from the game's map generator. Faint violet dashes: the three storm rings. Faint dots: the spawn ring and its spawn points. Your ship is the cyan circle (the real hull is drawn at true size inside it — it is tiny at this scale). Drag anywhere on the map to move it.

The shadow math has one number, K, that says how far you can see over an island's top before the curve of the sea hides a ship behind it. Bigger K: you see further past islands, so shadows are shorter.

3 · The display

Your radar screen, drawn like a real ship's radar on the game's chart: your ship in the middle, the real islands around it, a few made-up echoes. The sweep turns at the game's real speed. Zoom with the slider; the range rings re-space themselves so about five are on screen.

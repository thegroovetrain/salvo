Questions for Eric

  1. Proportions. Real hulls are 6.2 to 8.2 lengths per beam; today's battleship polygon is 3.9. Keep them to scale (beam multiplier 1.0), or fatten for readability? If fatten, pick the multiplier on the dial.
  2. Relative size. Warspite is 27 percent shorter than Iowa in real life. Keep the real sizes, or make all four the same length?
  3. Arc origin. Gunhouse center (as measured) or the barbette pivot about one percent of length toward amidships. It moves the wedge apex, nothing else.

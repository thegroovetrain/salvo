What each toggle decides

  • Offer corner (R4, open): where the always-live card group sits while a level is banked. Hover a card to see the consoles re-value; click a card to pin its preview.
  • Reload form (R6, render all three): the own hull from above with every mount at its real station, rows with one clock per turret and tube, or both.
  • Hull gauge (R7, open): a large percent, a segmented bar, the own hull tinting and cracking, or today's globe for comparison.
  • Surface (R12): floating 1 px linework over the water, or solid near-opaque edge strips.
  • In Offer pending the ship holds LIGHT TORPEDO II, NAVAL MINES I and SMOKE SCREEN I, so the four cards read their real next tiers; the preview on card 1 is applied at load.
  • The clocks cycle on a 10 s loop so the reload fill is visible; the real main-battery reload is the 9.5 s the console prints. Add ?still to the address to freeze them.

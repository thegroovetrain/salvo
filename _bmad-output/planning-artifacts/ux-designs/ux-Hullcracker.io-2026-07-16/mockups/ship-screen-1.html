Frame 1 — what is carried, what is new

Carried from shipdeck-1: the 1318 layer, the header with the account chip hard right, three stacked tiles with silhouette · name · pips · hue swatch, the hoist at the foot, the 718 px five-wide grid under a 70 px fade, CONFIRM SELECTION 50 px amber bottom-right, keys 1–3 / Enter / ESC.

  • Tile column 320 assumption — widened from 236 so the Shift line and three gun chips sit in the pip-label grid; the deck column's 280 px pays for it, the rest goes to the gutters. The grid does NOT grow to 6 wide.
  • Tile 320×204 assumption — silhouette left, four rows right: name · pips · SHIFT · GUN.
  • Shift line — the hull's FIXED ability (Eric 2026-09-21): SPEED BOOST · DAMAGE CUT · INSTANT RELOAD; label register = the pip labels; the glyphs are draft.
  • Gun picker — three chips in the account-chip register at 26 px assumption; DECK GUN preselected; the selected chip takes the hull's hue on the selected tile and silver on the others (the border is the non-colour channel); the pick rides the seat as gun.
  • Collection tile 130×72 assumption — glyph · 9 px name · lock state; no rail, nothing per copy. Hover raises the full 216×226 refit card (unchanged).
  • Hulls and guns are tiles too — the three unlock surfaces of Story 9.6; a hull's grid tile is its unlock, never a second picker assumption. The grouped rows are a reading aid assumption.
  • States shown once: DAZZLE locked (grey + lock at full alpha), PHOSPHOR pressed (amber + ⬢ 1 — the price is draft); on day one neither state occurs.

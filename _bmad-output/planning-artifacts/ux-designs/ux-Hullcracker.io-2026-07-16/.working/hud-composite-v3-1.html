What is on the bar

Left→right: HULL globe · Gun (keyless, 1.2 s wipe) · Shift (chamfer, 7 s wipe) · Q torpedo II · E machine gun I · R empty · belt 1–4 · helm globe.

Wipe geometry: transparent = elapsed, dark = remaining, uncovering clockwise from twelve. Gun 76% elapsed, Shift 65%.

REFIT WINDOW

Tab open over the same HUD, hotbar at 38%; four cards, keys 1–4, one fixed five-row face for every kind of card.

The face is ICON · NAME · TIER · KIND · five stat rows and nothing else — no activation wording, no sentences. Every card is the same height: the grid is sized to the catalog's largest card (NAVAL MINES, whose blast and trigger rings each take a row on top of reload, damage and mines held), and a card with fewer stats leaves its remaining rows blank. Tier rides the absolute loot ramp; cards without a tier (a consumable, an add-on) leave that line empty rather than inventing one. Card 1 is armed (hover); card 2 is refused in this illustrative state (all four belt slots full) — dual-coded with the reason word and a hollow key chip, never the dim alone.

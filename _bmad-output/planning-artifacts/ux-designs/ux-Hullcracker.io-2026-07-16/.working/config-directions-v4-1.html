Direction A — notes

  • Row grammar: label (10 px mono phosphor + a 9 px sub-label) · options as 150×64 cards: glyph · NAME · one 9 px stat line. A future CAPTAIN or STARTING KIT row is the same shape.
  • Shift is a read-only row today — one card, no siblings, "fixed for this hull" beside it; the day it becomes a pick, its siblings appear and the note goes. Nothing else moves.
  • Gun row: three cards, DECK GUN preselected; the pick rides the seat as gun.
  • The stat sub-lines on the cards are assumption — the refit card has no prose; one stat line may be too much. Say "names only" and they go.
  • Width: keeps the class layer's 1318; the column has ~1000 px of room for wider rows.
  • CONFIRM word open — Eric ("CONFIRM SELECTION" was the class pick's).

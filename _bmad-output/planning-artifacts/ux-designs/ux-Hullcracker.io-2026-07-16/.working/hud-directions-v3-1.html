Four bottom-bar directions for the nine-slot HUD

Composition boards for the in-match HUD rethink. Each direction is a complete 1366×768 frame (the ratified floor viewport, shown at 0.75×) with the same ocean, the same top chrome and the same mid-match Torpedo Boat state — only the bottom cluster, its density and its hierarchy change. Beside each frame: that direction's refit card face, redesigned so a player can tell what they are getting before they press 1–4. Palette, type family and the CIC register are fixed; nothing here proposes a new colour or word.

“The XP and HP bars shoved off to the side are a bit weird to read. I just think it needs to be cleaner … The chrome bar and kill feed are fine. Some players are also just picking refits randomly … so it needs better visual indication of what you're getting.” “I find myself looking all over during a fight.” “Diablo I/II, league of legends.” “I don't think columns is really working out. A hotbar at the bottom might work better. I need to visually see my abilities and cooldowns.” Eric · 2026-09-11 · .decision-log.md § Update 2026-09-10 (verbatim brief)

A

SUMMONER'S RIFT

Personality — the LoL register: one bar, everything you own in a line, cooldowns you can count.

Vitals and abilities become one bottom-centre object: two stacked horizontal bars (HULL, then XP) span the slot row's exact width, so the eye that checks a cooldown is already on the health bar. The slot row reads Gun · Shift · gap · Q E R · gap · 1 2 3 4 with the gaps as visible hairline ties, so the three families are legible without a label. Cooldown is the LoL radial: a dark clock-wipe uncovering clockwise with a large seconds numeral — the fastest count-read of the four. The helm stays a compact instrument bottom-right, unchanged in grammar.

B

TRISTRAM

Personality — the Diablo II register: two globes, a belt, and an experience strip under everything.

Health becomes a globe at the bar's left end — a filled arc reads as a quantity from the corner of the eye without numerals, the numerals are there when you look — and the helm becomes its mirror globe on the right, HDG large, KTS beneath, the 9-detent telegraph drawn as a tick arc over its crown and the rudder as a track along its floor. The four consumables sit in a framed belt as a distinct object, smaller than the weapons. XP is the thin strip running the bar's full width beneath the slots, LV at its head and the banked chip at its tail. Cooldown is the Diablo icon: dimmed to scrim, refilling bottom-up, with a small numeral.

C

BRIDGE CONSOLE

Personality — the naval-CIC take: one continuous instrument strip, helm hand left, gun hand right, every number present.

The whole bottom edge becomes one instrument strip read left to right in the order the hands sit: the helm (telegraph ladder, rudder, HDG/KTS) at the far left, then Gun · Shift, then Q E R, then 1–4, then LV and the bank at the seam, then HULL and XP as vertical gauges inside the bar at the far right. Every slot carries its name and a one-line quick stat, and cooldown is a thin phosphor bar that drains and refills under the slot plus a numeral — the same gauge grammar as the vitals, so the strip has one visual language. This is the densest, most information-forward direction; E shows the MACHINE GUN's held-fire state, its pool draining in amber.

D

CLEAN DECK

Personality — the minimal register: the smallest cluster that still shows everything, and no more.

One bar, one row. The bar is combined: HULL is the fill and XP is a hairline under it in the same box, LV a small chip at its head and the bank a small chip at its tail. Below it, a single row: Shift · Q E R at 54 px, then the four consumables at 40 px so the two families read by size alone. The deck gun has no slot (it is always ready or about to be). Cooldown is the icon area itself filling as a clock wipe — no numeral until the last 3 s, when one appears (Q is shown at 2 s to make the case). The helm collapses to HDG/KTS text bottom-right with the telegraph as a tick ladder beside it.

## Overall verdict

The bar is the right shape and the spine now carries the attention-priority table, the HP-pulse cap and the text-safe rule that the first run demanded — none of that regresses. What v3 gets wrong is **arithmetic the spine writes down and then breaks**: the refit band's two placement constraints have no solution at 768 (`row.y ≤ 388` and `row.y > 392` at once); the spine's own "no mono below 9 px" floor is violated by four registers the spine itself specifies at 8 and 7 px; and two ratified reductions — the results LOADOUT row at ×.72 and the deck card at ×.6 — push the ONLY tier read and the ONLY copies read to 5.8 px and 4.8 px respectively. The one genuinely structural finding is that a bottom-centre bar 790 × 128 px sits over aimable water on a viewport whose sight bubble is 192 px tall: at the 1.5× zoom ceiling the bar cuts into the sight bubble, the aim reticle is z-ordered under it, and the shipped hotbar gate swallows clicks on it — a Mine Layer's rear placement arc is the casualty. All of it is fixable at spec level; nothing requires abandoning a ratified direction.

**Counts: 0 critical · 7 high · 8 medium · 7 low** (3 of the 22 pre-existing). Eight items are *unverified* — specified but shown in no mock — and are listed as such, not failed.

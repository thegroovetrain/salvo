## Brainstorming Approach

**Selected Mode:** Guided (revised by advanced elicitation)

**Technique sequence:**
1. **Reference Blending / Analogical Thinking** — the spine. Real and historical naval weapons, sensors, countermeasures, damage control, propulsion and aviation, organized by the game's six verbs (**hurt, deny, see, hide, move, endure**) with eras as texture. Two standing questions on every find: *what countered this, and does the counter make a card?* (Emergence Engineering — measure/countermeasure pairs are the add-on generator) and *which shipped equipment does this substitute, split, or absorb?* (SCAMPER folded in; the shipped seven may change).
2. **Verbs Before Nouns** — each find becomes a card verb, not a museum exhibit.
3. **Morphological Analysis** — verb × tier bundle × add-on grid, fanning each verb into a ladder line (five bundles) and its add-ons.
4. **Player Fantasy Mining** — a per-hull coverage gate: does each starter fantasy (TB zip + torps, BS artillery + illumination, ML denial + sensors) have enough lines across all six verbs to build several distinct decks?
5. **Failure State Design** — how ships actually died (flooding, fire, magazine, steering, power) as the source of universal ship ladders and endure add-ons.
6. **Constraint Box** — the final filter: perception invariant, "land is sacred", no in-weapon selectors, three equipment lines per deck, every line needs a bot use rule.

**Focus Areas:**
- The rest of catalog v3: ladder lines (five authored tier bundles, copy 1 fills a slot) and add-ons (one copy, a stacking verb) per hull, toward ~100 cards / ~25–30 lines per hull, plus universal ship and gun lines.
- Consumables are OUT OF SCOPE: settled at three for launch (self-heal / short shield / longer damage-reduction buff), by Eric's ruling this session.
- Torpedo veto narrowed to within-a-weapon: separate torpedo lines are allowed; in-weapon selectors stay banned.
- Standing facts from Eric this session: the shipped equipment may change ("to some extent"); the current Torpedo is a HEAVY TORPEDO by its stats, so a Light Torpedo line is implied.
- Any hull may run any line (focus, not exclusivity); add-ons stack; class identity never depends on a variant behavior.

## Technique 6: Constraint Box — where a constraint bites
- **SMOKE SCREEN** = the first non-island SIGHT occluder; `losClear` (one binary test, six callers) must learn a second occluder. Largest single system on the list.
- **WAKE DRAFTING** = a per-tick kinematics change → shared sim + client prediction; the client synthesizes in-bubble wakes from contact pose so the data exists, but architecture owns the desync risk.
- **LAUNCHER-GATED CONSUMABLES** (Shkval round) = a second grey-out clause on the existing "full slots grey the card" offer rule.
- **CHAFF** must go through the one `blipGate` / `blipShape` like jamming fakes; the ledgered "fakes not water-filtered" defect now applies to an aimable round.
- **DRIFTING MINES** need the currents system + a water filter.
- **BOT USE RULES**: 15 lines = 15 tactics (the tactic table is total); HARPOON and SMOKE have no obvious bot behavior yet.
- Clean: Shift is unbound; vertical missiles overflying islands has the cannon precedent; no in-weapon selectors; every starter is exactly 3 lines.

## Themes and Patterns
- **Splits by real calibre and launch method make lines for free** — Heavy/Light/Super torpedo and horizontal/vertical missile are the same trick: arc vs freedom, speed vs warning.
- **Every counter must also do something on its own** — Eric's BR-deckbuilding filter.
- **Arcade, not simulation** — no endure column, no fire, no armor model, no crit zones.
- **Tiers are stats, verbs are add-ons** — a verb never appears on a ladder.
- **Special ordnance through a launcher you carry** — a consumable category with no new keys or slots.
- **Breadth is solved; add-ons are the gap** — 15 lines × 5 + universals clears ~100 before consumables.

## Session Summary

### Most Promising Concepts

**Top Pick: The 15-line equipment catalog, mined from real ships**
Breadth is solved in one session: 15 lines × 5 tiers plus universals clears the ~100-card target before consumables. Every line came from a real weapon with a real launch method, so the arcs, speeds and starters have a source Eric can check rather than a number someone made up.

A consumable that fires through equipment you already carry (the Shkval round through Heavy tubes). Opens a whole consumable family with no new keys, slots, selectors or doctrine conflicts, and it fell out of Eric's own "consumable that needs Heavy tubes" instinct.

**Honorable Mention: The universal ability layer**
Speed boost leaves the equipment list for Shift, the radar buoy leaves it for a consumable slot, and every hull's three slots are now purely a build. The anchor may join Shift.

### Key Insights
- Every counter card must also do something on its own (BR + deckbuilding kills narrow counters).

- Splitting by real calibre and launch method (Heavy/Light/Super, horizontal/vertical) makes lines for free.
- Universal Shift boost voids balance cycle 1's numbers; rerun before authoring tiers.

### Thread 1 — The First-Minute Objective

**[Objective #1]**: Four valid verbs
_Core Loop_: At 0:05 with the drop rings on the chart a captain may race the nearest ring, pick between rings, stalk a zone's edge, or bait by letting someone else go first. The zone must support all four equally.
_Novelty_: No scripted "correct" opening; the opening is a read of the other captains. (Technique: Verbs Before Nouns)

**[Objective #2]**: Routes, not a funnel
_Core Loop_: The crate pulls hard enough to entice, but passive XP (1 level/min), the minute crates, and plain hunting remain viable routes. Skipping every zone is a route, not a loss.
_Novelty_: The objective is one lane of several. Pins a later number question: crate value vs the passive drip. (Eric)

**[Objective #3]**: One crate per zone, three nested circles
_Core Loop_: The marked drop zone (big ring, on the chart from the cycle's 0:00) contains a smaller crate zone, which contains the crate (appears at 1:00). (Eric)

**[Objective #4]**: Fresh crate pays most, then sinks
_Core Loop_: Payout is per tick while a hull sits in the crate zone; leaving keeps what was banked. Rate is highest at 1:00 and falls as the crate visibly sinks, reaching zero about when the ring starts closing (3:00 of the cycle). The sinking hull is the timer.
_Novelty_: "Opens over time" chosen over sail-over / hold-alone / siege / tow. Partial credit for a contested grab; the zone empties itself before the storm. (Eric; Technique: Reward Schedule)

**[Objective #5]**: Guards are optional
_Core Loop_: Build and test the crate alone; guards return only if zones prove too safe. (Eric)

Open at this point: two-hulls-in-the-crate-zone rule (share / split / stall); what the outer ring does if there are no guards (signpost vs "you are lit in here"); what the ticks pay (XP only vs consumable milestone vs stat bumps).

**[Objective #6]**: Share or split, never stall
_Core Loop_: Two hulls in range both farm at full rate, or split the tick; no second-hull lock. (Eric; resolved at #12: full rate)

**[Objective #7]**: No outer ring
_Core Loop_: Only the beacon location is telegraphed. If guards return they operate on range from the beacon; no drawn domain. (Eric)

**[Objective #8]**: XP straight into the meter; it's a RADIO BEACON, not a crate
_Novelty_: The rename gives the decay a fiction — the signal fades, the beacon sinks. (Eric)

**[Objective #9]**: On the chart because it broadcasts
_Core Loop_: Every captain hears the beacon at the cycle's 0:00; that is the whole telegraph. (Eric)

**[Objective #10]**: Farming is not loud
_Core Loop_: No extra paint for a receiving hull; the known location + ordinary radar is exposure enough. (Eric; rejected: "receiving marks you")

**[Objective #11]**: Signal range 500 u
_Core Loop_: A circle of 500 u (sight-bubble width) around the beacon is the farm zone. (Eric, provisional number)

**[Objective #12]**: Full rate for every hull in range
_Core Loop_: A signal doesn't divide; everyone in range receives the full tick. Tweak if it sucks. (Eric)

Open: without drones, every radar blip near a beacon is certainly a captain — keep radar noise some other way (guards-as-noise / beacon has its own return / flotsam blips) or accept the honesty?

Radar-noise question: Eric undecided (2026-11-03) — parked as an OPEN THREAD with four options: guards-as-noise, beacon has its own radar return, airdrop blips, or accept that every blip is a captain.

## Themes and Patterns

- **Routes, not funnels.** Beacon, airdrops, and hunting are three lanes; the hoarder is meant to be a real style.
- **Identity from layout and rhythm, not new stats.** 3×3 vs 4×2 turrets, fore/aft arcs, per-turret cooldowns and arc-click-reload produce four distinct ships before a single stat is set.
- **One number system.** Cards (+20 flat), airdrops (+1..+5), and hull identity (offsets from Bismarck = 100) all land in the same indexes; 1000 HP everywhere; effective HP is the hidden truth.
- **Hold the card system still.** Cards draw, fill slots, and tier up as today; the ship and the ocean carry the new weight.
- **The zone rhythm already fits.** Beat 2 (SUPPLY, reserved no-op) is the beacon's surfacing minute; beat 3 (reveal) is its exit cue; the close destroys uncollected airdrops.
- **Subtract.** Deck guns tabled, consumables dropped, buoys/decoys/chaff/shield gone, drones gone; two universal cooldowns (Hull Repair, Smoke) replace the kit.

## Promising Combinations

- **Beacon × ring reveal**: the 2:00 reveal is the "stay or run" decision for every farmer — no new timer needed.
- **Fore/aft arcs × per-turret cooldowns**: turning to bring a fresh turret into arc is the core skill move; bow-on charges cost 4×2 ships more than 3×3.
- **Secondaries × mines**: right-click sweeping gives every hull a mine answer, which is what lets mines be one chosen kind per slot.
- **Index model × airdrops × equal DPS**: Power × Reload ≈ 10,000 keeps output flat while +1..+5 drops and +20 cards push identity around on one scale.
- **Wreckage × third parties**: a sunk ship's bundle draws a late arrival to every fight, feeding the beacon standoff with a reason to break.

## Session Summary

### Most Promising Concepts

**Top Pick: The Radio Beacon (Story 10.2's objective)**
One beacon per zone (8/4/2 per cycle), global on the chart from the cycle's 0:00 because it broadcasts, surfaces at 1:00 (the reserved SUPPLY beat), pays XP per tick to every hull within 500 u at full rate starting at 2 levels/min, fades and sinks toward the close; placed by the fleet-spawn rule; no guards to start. It slots into the existing zone rhythm with no new timer, supports all four opening verbs, and is small enough to build and test as one story.

**Runner-up: Four Historical Battleships with Their Real Guns**
Yamato, Bismarck, HMS Warspite, USS Iowa replace the three classes. The ship IS the armament; deck guns tabled. Per-turret cooldowns + fore/aft arcs + arc-click-reload give 3×3 and 4×2 hulls distinct rhythms before any stat is set. Secondaries on right click (short, light, sweep mines). Shift is a free pre-queue pick from boost / hunker / instant reload. Three slots (torpedo, mine, special shell) filled only by cards, one kind each, tiered as today. Consumables dropped; Hull Repair (C) and Smoke (V) are universal cooldowns.

**Honorable Mention: The Index Stat Model**
1000 HP on every hull shown as 0–100 %; every stat an index around 100 with Bismarck as the 100 ship (Armor, Power, Reload, Engines, Intel = sweep RPM/15, Range); equal main-gun DPS so Power × Reload ≈ 10,000; cards +20 flat, airdrops +1..+5, one scale; effective HP is the hidden truth. Plus the airdrops themselves: 2 per minute remaining to 12:00 (156), biased away from beacons, sighted-only on the chart, sail-over, permanent, storm-destroyed, wreckage leaves a bundle.

### Key Insights

- The zone's four-beat rhythm already had the beacon's shape in it (SUPPLY beat 2 empty, reveal at beat 3 as the exit cue).
- Removing drones makes every radar blip a captain — a real change to the radar game, deliberately deferred until the slice is tested.
- Turret layout and arcs produce ship identity for free; the stat table is the second lever, not the first.
- Holding the card system still (draw, fill, tier-up as today) keeps the rework bounded to ship + ocean + kit.
- Subtraction was most of the session: deck guns, consumables, buoys, decoys, chaff, shield, drones, the outer ring, guards (for now).

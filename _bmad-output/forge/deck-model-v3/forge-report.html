The Deck Model v3

Forge report · Hullcracker.io · Eric · 2026-09-02 · outcome: HARDENED

One unit, on Eric's ruling "go big or go home": accounts plus server-held, per-hull, authored 40-card decks; four consumable slots instead of a hand; heal as a scarce stockable card; gun-only spawns with a weighted, mulligan-able opening draw; a deck editor; placement-scaled unlock tokens; match history as the live harness. Input was the 2026-09-01 brainstorm (82 ideas, ten bench rulings) diffed against the 2026-08-27 equipment rework, whose paused forge this one supersedes in part.

Locked decisions

  1. Tab is untouched. Level → four different lines from your deck → pick one. One level buys any card. No hand, no second clock, no storefront.
  2. Draw rule: equal per-card weight, no pity, no class weighting. Measured: a one-copy line is offered at least once in 66% of 8-pick matches and 90% at 15 — the small deck does the pity's work. Composition carries the class tilt.
  3. Card model (A): a weapon is one ladder line (copy 1 = the weapon; copies 2–5 = tier bundles) plus separate one-copy add-ons drawable any time. Universal lines are ladders too. Copies are the tier ceiling. Add-ons target a weapon family (tags).
  4. Legal deck: exactly 40 (dials; a 25–40 band must be tested), copies ≤ line max, every card unlocked, hull as a label only, no composition rules, frozen at queue, starter decks pass the same check.
  5. Consumable slots: four, keys 1–4 with Tab closed. Full slots grey the card and the server refuses the pick; the immutable offer is the "use one, come back" mechanism. Leaves the deck on pick. Server-owned state. Engine supports key-fires and key-primes-then-click; content is open.
  6. Spawn: deck gun only. Level zero at countdown start; one mulligan, countdown only; weighted first draw guarantees something usable. Pinned card is a later experiment.
  7. Heal: the 5 key dies; DAMAGE CONTROL is a consumable card at the shipped 100 hp, 4–5 copies per starter [DRAFT]. The sudden-death heal question closes by construction. Passive per-level heal parked.
  8. Account: no guest tier. Anonymous = today's game on the starter deck, nothing stored. Signed in via OAuth only (Google/Discord, minimal scopes, never an email or password) = decks, unlocks, tokens, history. Same rules either way — signing in changes what you keep.
  9. Unlocks and earn: a whole line per unlock, flat price [DRAFT], any order; account level → one token per level; XP placement-scaled, Solo vs AI discounted; matches-to-full-catalog is an open dial, 40–60 band [DRAFT].
  10. Bots carry authored 40-card decks in the player format, a total ConsumableTactic record, and the harness runs authored and random-legal arms with six pinned bars.
  11. Enemy decks are shown to no player and recorded server-side for Eric's metrics; players see their own deck in results and history.
  12. Catalog breadth [DRAFT]: a hull builds from ~100 cards' worth of unlockables. The catalog is Eric's document.

Weak points that survived scrutiny

  • The identity fork's "promise" is now "something to do at 0:00," not "your class weapon at 0:00." Samus lost that fight; the weighted draw has to earn it on the water.
  • A third of median-length matches never show a given one-copy doctrine (66%). Accepted as what "one copy" means, not patched.
  • Anonymous players earn nothing. Either a hook or a leak; only live numbers will say.
  • The account is the project's first database and first non-ops HTTP API, and Story 7-7 stays deferred. Architecture owes this a shape.
  • Every number marked [DRAFT] is a placeholder with a dial and a harness bar, not a ruling.

Outcome

Hardened, on the second pass. The first pass closed after six questions without challenging a premise; Eric reopened it. The second pass put six unchallenged premises in front of eight independent agents, who returned one verdict: every premise was cracked or dead as written, and the common fault was that nothing had been named as broken. Eric then ruled each premise in his own words. The slice stands, reshaped: the beacon pot splits, the index model's costs are accepted and ramming rethought, all four hulls are battleships on purpose with a Rudder stat, the airdrops are flavor, and healing stays scarce.

Sequencing

The whole slice goes to correct-course as one piece, later, not this session. The beacons-first Story 10.2 cut from the first pass is withdrawn.

The six premises, attacked and ruled

1. Drones are an anti-pattern. Room: the record held one sentence ("fairly uninteresting") and no match data. Eric: they work as built; the map feels busy, you can't tell player from drone, you still hunt them, and they don't pull players into conflict with each other. Beacons are a hypothesis to test. Guards are the fallback.
2. Beacons give four verbs. Room: max-min placement puts each beacon equidistant from two captains; full-rate sharing and a blessed standoff make "sit and truce" the only verb. Eric: the pot splits. King-of-the-hill rejected. Build 8/4/2; 6/4/2 tabled.
3. The index model fixes something. Room split: a hidden multiply and dead ramming mass, or a readout rename. The "ARMOR card worth halves" claim was wrong (the gate nets Armor minus Power). Ramming mass = max hull was verified. Eric: it is both a tuning ruler and readability; costs accepted; ramming mass is probably Armor; the HUD shows whole-number percentages only.
4. Four battleships belong here. Room: the fast-and-fragile verb (1.29× speed, 2× turn, 300 hp) has no hull; Iowa is a quicker tank. Eric: gone on purpose; stats decide glass vs beef; Rudder is a new stat for turn rate.
5. 156 airdrops are decisions. Room: a drop is a sixth of a card; a 1,000 u detour costs half a card of passive time; nobody turns. Eric: keep 156 as flavor; the hoarder style is dropped.
6. Dropping the kit five days after building it. Room: no recorded failure; a timer heal silently overturns "healing scarcity prices recklessness." Eric: too many buttons, not broken; healing stays scarce with a long Hull Repair cooldown.

Also locked

  • Lasting ship identity is layout only; stats are a head start cards and drops may erase.
  • Secondaries uniform on every hull; rhythm belongs to the player; mines hurt by secondaries only.
  • Airdrops paint on radar by imprint. Passive healing and the HULL POINTS ladder die.
  • Right click is secondaries-or-cancel, intended.

Rejected

  • Scaling cards, wider offsets, Reload out of the index. Per-hull secondaries, three ships, a destroyer/cruiser/battleship mix.
  • Nearest-hull beacons. Drops worth a card, fewer-bigger-exclusive, drops with a downside.
  • Passive healing beside Hull Repair. Any shell pops a mine.

Weak points that survived

  • Bismarck and Warspite are one ship once the head start fades.
  • Equal DPS is paper-equal; the tank wins straight trades ~1.7×. Intended.
  • Shift free pick has dominant pairs; numbers after staging.
  • The kit retire undoes Stories 10.1/10.9.

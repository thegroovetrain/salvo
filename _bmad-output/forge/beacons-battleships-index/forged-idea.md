## The six premises, ruled
1. **Drones.** They work as built. Retired because the map feels busy (can't tell player from drone), you still have to hunt them, and they do not draw players into conflict with each other. Beacons are a hypothesis to test against that. Drones may return as beacon GUARDS if beacons alone are good but boring; a few passive targets also possible.
2. **Beacons.** Built at **8/4/2**. **The pot splits**: one tick shared by every hull in range (two get half, three a third); fighting, not fighting, and sharing are all real options (#12 full-rate overturned). "Only the nearest hull receives" rejected. Fewer beacons (6/4/2) tabled for later.
3. **Index model** (1000 HP, every stat ≈ 100, Bismarck = 100). It is **both** a one-ruler tuning scale and player readability. Costs accepted: HULL POINTS dies, every weapon damage re-ruled as % of 1000, Reload becomes multiplicative, "we'll rescale everything." **Ramming mass under flat HP = Armor** (probably); the 9.5 energy law keeps its shape. **HUD shows whole-number 0–100 % only**; nobody ever sees a raw figure out of 1000.
4. **Four battleships.** The fast-and-fragile verb is **gone on purpose**; all four are battleships; stats decide glass-cannon vs beefcake. **RUDDER** is a new index stat for turn rate; Engines does not include it. Mixed ship types rejected.
5. **Airdrops.** **156 stay, +1..+5, as flavor**: litter on purpose, taken when on your line, never a route; "the hoarder is a viable style" dropped. They paint on radar by imprint (small, stationary = crate).
6. **Kit.** Too many buttons, not broken. **Healing stays scarce**: Hull Repair's cooldown is LONG (longer than a typical fight, a minute or more; exact number is the harness's), so pressing early is a mistake. Passive healing dies (regen + ladder). Identity-fork lock 4 stands.

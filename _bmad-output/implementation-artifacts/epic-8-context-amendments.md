## Story 8.3 — The Draw (cycle 138, 2026-09-15)

13. **THE DRAW MODEL IS CONFIRMED AS "RESHUFFLE AFTER EVERY DRAW".** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion). Eric's words: *"fairly from what is left in the deck"* means the deck reshuffles after every draw — it does NOT need to exhaust before unchosen cards re-enter the pool. The shipped model already does this: a draw READS the pool at full weight and only a FIT removes one card, so a passed-on card is back at full weight next level with no exhaustion step in between. Story 8.3 pins this reading and adds the missing pieces (the at-cap guard, the once-per-ship exhaustion callback, the `/metrics` counter); no new draw engine is built. **Supersedes** nothing; confirms `epic-8-context.md`'s "Draw economy" paragraph as already correct and closes the ambiguity a fresh reader could take from "fairly from what is left."

14. **AN EXHAUSTED DECK PRESENTS NO OPTIONS, BUT THE LEVEL STILL BANKS.** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion). Eric's words: *"When you have expended all your cards, you are no longer presented with options."* An empty draw banks the level (the chip counts it), materializes no offer, queues no `pt` event, and shows no TAB cue. It stays spendable only via the menu heal — client-unreachable today (`deferred-work.md:982`) — until Story 8.8 makes heal a card. The "still banks" option was taken; rejected: "does not bank" and "open the band with heal only". **Supersedes** nothing; resolves the open re-derivation question left at `deferred-work.md:1995`.

15. **CORRECTION OF RECORD, DEFERRED TO STORY 8.8.** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion). Eric's words: the free per-level heal "has been gone a while, it was replaced with a 1% missing HP per second while out of combat (over 30s since the last time you took damage)." Measured fact: `development` at `b18fba6` still carries `CONFIG.damageControl.levelMissingPct: 0.1` and `levelRegenMs: 5000` — the per-level, 10%-of-missing-hp-over-5-seconds heal — and no out-of-combat regen mechanism exists anywhere in shipped code or in any planning doc. Story 8.3 does NOT touch the level heal; Story 8.8's AC already carries a "check which version is live" clause, and that check must be resolved WITH Eric before either version is built. **Supersedes** nothing; ledgers the discrepancy for Story 8.8 (see `deferred-work.md`'s 2026-09-15 Story 8.3 section).

## Story 8.4 — The Damage Gate and the Ordnance Collector (cycle 139, 2026-09-15)

16. **SHELLS AND BURSTS DETONATE ANY ARMED MINE; CAPTIVE MINES STAY IMMUNE.** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion). "Shooting ANY mine detonates it" (FR57, AR44) is read as: a shell or a burst sets off any mine on the water — the shooter's own or anyone else's — provided the mine has finished its arm delay; a mine still arming is not set off by a shell, and a CAPTIVE mine (the torpedo-launching kind, R2.18 / epic-7 ruling 2026-08-19) is never set off by a shell or a burst. Rejected: "any mine, armed or not, captive included"; "any armed mine, captive included". **Supersedes** the shipped owner-only gate in `detonateMinesInBurst` (own mines were the ONLY mines a burst could detonate); keeps the R2.18 captive carve-out.

17. **A MINE IS A POINT TARGET FOR A SHELL.** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion, recommended option). A shell detonates a mine when its path passes within the SHELL's own radius (`CONFIG.<gun>.shellRadius`) of the mine's centre; no new mine-size number exists. A burst detonates a mine when the burst radius covers the mine's centre (unchanged rule). Rejected: the mine's trigger radius; a new CONFIG hit radius.

18. **A MINE BLAST CHAINS INTO ANY MINE IN RANGE, WHOEVER LAID IT.** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion, recommended option). One detonation sets off every ARMED, non-captive mine within its blast radius regardless of owner, resolving the same tick through the existing visited set. Rejected: same-owner chains only (the shipped rule). Captive mines keep R2.18 in full: they neither receive nor propagate a chain (consistent with amendment 16). **Supersedes** the same-owner condition in `chainMines`.

19. **A BURST OVER A HULL THAT SANK THIS TICK MARKS `hc`, NOT `sp`.** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion, recommended option) resolving `deferred-work.md` `:590`. When a multi-barrel click's first shell sinks a hull and a later shell of the same tick bursts over the wreck, the burst counts the wreck as a GEOMETRIC victim for the hit-call mark only: `hc` is emitted, no damage is dealt, nothing else changes. The zero-damage star shell keeps its exclusion (geometric victims never count for it — no unsanctioned detection channel). Rejected: keep `sp` and pin it as accepted. Refines the Story 4.3 `hc`/`sp` grammar (epic-4 amendments 16–18) for this one case.

20. **A MINE IS NEVER A COLLISION SUBJECT FOR A SHELL IN FLIGHT — ONLY THE BURST AT THE CLICKED POINT CAN SET ONE OFF.** Eric ruling 2026-09-16, verbatim: *"If I did not DIRECTLY click on the mine, then UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES WHATSOEVER SHOULD IT BLOCK A SHOT, REGISTER A HIT OR MISS, OR GIVE ANY INDICATION WHATSOEVER TO THE SHOOTER THAT ANYTHING MIGHT BE THERE. AT ALL."* A shell passing over a mine on its way to the aim point flies on untouched: the mine does not detonate, the shell is not consumed, no boom, no `hc`, no `sp`, nothing. A mine detonates by gunfire ONLY when the burst at the point the shooter clicked covers the mine's centre (the click IS the shooter's own information; the resulting mine boom stays sight-gated). Amendment 16's "shell or burst" therefore reads "burst"; amendment 18 (chains) and the captive/arming carve-outs stand. **Supersedes** amendment 17 (the in-flight point-target rule) in full and the Story 8.4 review ruling that a mine-consumed shell emits no mark (there is no mine-consumed shell any more).

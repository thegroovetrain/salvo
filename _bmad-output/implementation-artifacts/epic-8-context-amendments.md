## Story 8.0 — Colyseus 0.18 Upgrade (cycle 134, 2026-09-14)

1. **`@colyseus/core` 0.18.13 and `@colyseus/schema` 5.0.32, not the AC's 0.18.12 / 5.0.27.** Eric ruling 2026-09-14 (AskUserQuestion, "Latest patches"). The story's pinned target set was registry-verified on 2026-09-09; core 0.18.13 shipped 2026-09-12 as a security fix in exactly the area the staging gate lives (*a room's `onAuth()` and plugin `onAuth` hooks now run when a client joins with a valid `@colyseus/auth` token*), and schema had moved to 5.0.32. Eric took the latest patches under his standing *"keep everything CURRENT"* ruling. **Supersedes** the version literals in epics.md Story 8.0 AC, AR19, and game-architecture.md D10 for those two packages ONLY; every other package installs at the version the AC lists (`colyseus` 0.18.5, `@colyseus/tools` 0.18.3, `@colyseus/sdk` 0.18.2, `@colyseus/auth` 0.18.2, `@colyseus/database` 0.18.3, `@colyseus/admin` 0.18.5, `@colyseus/monitor` 0.18.3, `@colyseus/playground` 0.18.4). The actually-resolved set is recorded in `spec-8-0-colyseus-0-18-upgrade.md`.

2. **The three unmounted Epic 9 modules (`@colyseus/auth`, `@colyseus/database`, `@colyseus/admin`) live in `server/package.json` `dependencies`, not `devDependencies`.** Eric ruling 2026-09-14. They are production modules in Epic 9; installing them now proves the whole 0.18 set (including the `drizzle-orm` 1.0.0-rc.2 pre-release inside `@colyseus/database`) resolves together before any account code exists. Accepted cost: a few unused packages in the production image until Story 9.1 mounts them. Nothing is mounted, routed, or configured by Story 8.0 — no env var, no `render.yaml` change.

3. **`postgres` and `drizzle-kit` are DEFERRED to Story 9.1 — Story 8.0 does not install them.** Eric ruling 2026-09-14. **Supersedes** the *"`postgres` 3.4.9 and `drizzle-kit` 0.31.10 present"* clause of the Story 8.0 AC and the matching line in AR19 / D10. Reason: nothing in 8.0 runs a migration or opens a database socket, so "present" bought nothing; and the AC's `drizzle-kit` 0.31.10 is the 0.44-era kit while `@colyseus/database` 0.18.3 hard-pins `drizzle-orm` 1.0.0-rc.2, whose matching kit is the `1.0.0-rc` line — installing a mismatched kit now would have handed 9.1 a trap. Story 9.1 installs both at the versions verified against the ORM the module actually ships with; the mismatch is ledgered in `deferred-work.md`.

## Story 8.1 — The Card Model and Catalog Engine (cycle 135, 2026-09-15)

5. **Interim state between 8.1 and the content stories is "stay playable".** Eric ruling 2026-09-15 (AskUserQuestion, recommended option). Story 8.1 ships an interim deck of every catalog line whose mechanism already exists (the five ladders and the deck-gun family with catalog-v3 numbers; `heavyTorpedo` / `navalMines` / `broadside` / `starShells` slot fills; the add-ons whose doctrine verbs exist). Lines for weapons and consumables that later stories build are authored in full shape (copy 1 + empty tiers) but carry `stub: true` and are EXCLUDED from `buildDeck()` and therefore from every offer until their story flips the flag. Rejected: "engine only, degraded staging" and "author every v3 tier now". **Supersedes** nothing; fills a gap the epics leave open (the AC allows stubbing but says nothing about the draw pool).

Rejected: `torpedo` → `lightTorpedo`; keeping legacy ids until 8.13.

8. **Interim refit card (until 8.6/8.7): kind word in neutral colour.** Eric ruling 2026-09-15. Rarity and category die in 8.1; the CURRENT refit card's meta row shows the v3 kind word (`WEAPON · UPGRADE · ADD-ON · CONSUMABLE`) plus the copy count, no tier-ramp colour, and the hotbar's per-slot `◆n` category count is removed.

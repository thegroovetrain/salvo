## What Went Well

1. **The spec contract was perfect under maximum design churn: 0 intent gaps, 0 bad-spec across 10 stories** — through a mid-epic wholesale redesign (weighted rolls → THE DECK MODEL), a control-scheme supersession (SPACE-hold → TAB), and 57 amendments. The specs never pointed at the wrong target.
2. **The amendment protocol is the design organ that works.** Every story ran pre-implementation AskUserQuestion rulings; supersession chains (ESC law 21→22→23) show design converging *during* execution without breakage; the regeneration-proof amendments file (Epic 1 Action #1) is treated as the true design source.
3. **The handwave→evidence pipeline held end-to-end.** Every story declared its values as implementer handwaves inside ratified pins; 2.10's evidence checkpoint (amendment 55 — a hard halt on autonomy) ratified CONFIG.xp as-is and moved exactly one dial (`deck.rareWeightPerDryLevel` 0.35→0.7, flat-pity finding) with seeded, byte-reproducible evidence.
4. **Architecture discipline paid a direct dividend:** the zero-Colyseus, wall-clock-free World let 2.10 run the real sim in-process at ~8 matches/sec (1,785 ticks of a full match in 121ms) — the batch-sim harness exists *because* the Epic 0 walls held.
5. **The review gate stayed calibrated to risk:** finding density tracked surface novelty (2.3 DOM overlays: 21 patches/7 highs; 2.8 new mechanics: 14 patches/1 high; 2.10 production surfaces: clean — all findings were harness tooling). Every high was caught pre-merge. Cross-model review earned unique catches in ≥6 stories (reconnect stale-prime, control-char name spoofing, torpU-track gap, burn direction, departed-captain crash) and was adjudicated-wrong twice with recorded rationale.
6. **Structural CI-as-policy tests** turned design law into build failures: damageGuardrail salvo pin (2.8), fitCheck catalog walk (2.9), pts===offers invariant, byte-identical reproducibility (2.10), simulated-deuteranopia distinguishability (2.3), hook registry-lock (2.5).
7. **The ledger routed bidirectionally and verifiably:** entries opened in one story closed in a named later story (2.6→2.7 dead-killer toast; 2.5→2.8 validation; 2.7→2.8 band overlap; 2.8→2.9 presentation seams; 2.10→3.1 picks band).

## Challenges & Growth Areas

1. **Same-tick concurrency is the house bug family** — passive-bank-mid-spend (2.6, 2.7), same-tick mine double-detonation and scrub-to-empty deadlock (2.8), burn-at-expiry-tick (2.9). Concurrency-within-a-tick, not races. Now a standing review line.
2. **Lifecycle staleness after reconnect/death/respawn appeared in every story 2.1–2.5** (stale prime, stale hotbar post-forceSnap, wiped placement latch, glyph-fade replay, respawn boon semantics) — Epic 1 named this family; it remains the most reliable bug well. Codex found it twice independently.
3. **Inference-of-success failed twice before explicit ACK ended it** (2.6→2.7 spend-latch lineage). Lesson generalized: prefer explicit acknowledgment over state-delta inference.
4. **"Fail-closed" was the dominant patch verb** — prototype-key resolution (`resolveBoons(['constructor'])`), NaN/degenerate-numeric guards in five stories, CLI foot-guns (2.10's `Number('')===0`, `offer.size=0` infinite loop). Degenerate-input guarding should be a first-write habit, not a review catch.
5. **The one sim-code HIGH of the epic:** 2.8's triple-barrel one-click kill (3×25 > 70hp) defeating the ratified guardrail — emergent-interaction math needs explicit pins (the salvo guardrail test now exists).
6. **Every visual story shipped without a browser pass** (2.2/2.3/2.4/2.9 residuals). The pure-core/thin-Pixi-shell pattern makes this *possible*; the two real post-merge misses were both visual. Mitigation is now official: Eric's live-play eye is the acceptance mechanism (see Rulings).
7. **2.3 carried `[multiple-goals, oversized]` and paid for it** (21 patches, 7 highs) — the story-sizing creep flagged in Epics 0 and 1 continued.

## Key Insights

1. **Rule-up-front + correct-on-sight is the operating model.** Eric surfaces all questions before work; anything he dislikes he orders changed on sight. Docs true up in batches (7.5 owns the full refresh); the amendments file is the live source of truth. The system should never expect Eric to pre-author doc changes for mid-build ideas.
2. **Work routed through the pipeline gets done; free-floating obligations rot.** Consequence adopted: every future obligation gets a ledger entry with evidence and a named home, or it doesn't exist.
3. **The headline economy finding: match length, not XP dials, is the constraint.** Picks p50 0.72–2.31 vs the 12–20 band because matches end ~1:20 vs the ~12:00 ring-closed design. Ratified, routed to Story 3.1; no XP dial may chase the band before the phased timeline lands.
4. **Harness numbers carry honesty caveats:** match lengths are LOWER bounds (omniscient pilots); kill rates upper bounds; deck-only stopping-rule numbers are model-internal, never production lifetimes; zone-insensitivity holds only at current pilot lethality and may invert once the zone binds. Re-check economy conclusions with Epic 6 perception-honest bots.
5. **Catalog/registry content IS wire contract** (2.5 standing convention) — any entry change requires a PV bump, else stale clients silently desync.

## Rulings Made in This Retro (Eric, live, 2026-08-01)

1. **Follow-up-review flags: all 18 waived; flag retired as a category.** The flag (`followup_review_recommended`) was a residual-risk disclaimer with no consumption point anywhere in the workflow — zero production incidents from any flagged story; every high was caught pre-merge by the standing gate. Going forward: a reviewer who genuinely believes something needs a second look files a **deferred-work ledger entry with evidence and a named home** (the same contract as every other deferral), or files nothing. Retro-time flag audits end here.
2. **Storm purple: the exclusivity clause is dead.** Eric: what matters is that **players can easily see the edge of the ring against everything else on the water** — not that purple appears nowhere else. Accent reuse of storm purple by other systems (e.g. EXCLUSIVE boons, amendment 49) is explicitly fine. Story 3.2's "purple appears nowhere else in the game" AC clause is superseded; the binding requirement for 3.2's design gate is **ring-edge legibility** (current ring vs next ring vs fog vs blips, at any zoom). 3.2's spec must be authored from this ruling, not the stale clause.
3. **Eric's live-play eye is the official visual-acceptance mechanism.** The formal "recorded browser pass" requirement (Epic 0/1 retro carryover) is retired; the demonstrated loop — Eric plays, Eric orders fixes — is the mechanism of record.

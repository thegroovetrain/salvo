

## Acoustic homing standard — built 2026-11-16 (cycle 242, 0.19.36, PV 109 -> 110; `spec-acoustic-homing.md`)

20. **ACOUSTIC HOMING COMES STANDARD ON LIGHT, HEAVY AND CAPTIVE TORPEDOES; HEAVY RELOAD 40 S; LIGHT DAMAGE 250; IOWA ARMOR 95.** Eric 2026-11-16: *"Remove acoustic homing from the tiered upgrades. Acoustic homing instead comes standard on all Light and Heavy torpedoes. It is ACOUSTIC homing, meaning the louder a target within range is, the more the torpedo is attracted to it … tracking be standardized at whatever the max tracking is for current Tier 5 against a target with an ENGINES stat of 100 at full throttle, going up or down based on the target's engines and their current throttle setting"*; *"Captive mines should get the same tracking, just use their current Tier V tracking number as their baseline."*; *"Lets keep those torpedo changes. Lets change Iowa's ARMOR to 95."*
   - (a) **Rows:** light and heavy home from Tier I at `CONFIG.torpedo.homingTurnRate` (0.5 rad/s, the old Tier V); captive at `CONFIG.captiveMines.homingTurnRate` (0.3, its old Tier V); no tier steps homing any more. The supercav never homes.
   - (b) **Loudness** = target ENGINES / 100 × |throttle| (server `collectHulls`; reverse is as loud as ahead; Speed Boost adds nothing beyond full throttle). The fish turns at row rate × loudness; a silent hull (throttle 0) is never homed on. A free fish locks the LOUDEST legal target inside `homingAcquireRange` (nearer wins a tie), re-picked every tick; a captive fish keeps its pinned victim and only its turn follows the victim's noise. A radio beacon reads loudness 1 (still a legal lock, R12).
   - (c) **Numbers:** heavy torpedo reload 30 → 40 s; light torpedo base damage 200 → 250; Iowa ARMOR 85 → 95.
   - (d) **Evidence** (`batch-sim-evidence-2026-11-16.md`, section "Acoustic homing"; 91 matches an arm, random spend, 11.9 bots): acoustic alone — heavy level with supercav, light −7.3 at 4:00; acoustic + heavy 40 s + light 250 + Iowa ARMOR 100 — torpedo kills per holder 0.31 / 0.40 / 0.39, all three level at 4:00, supercav +4 at 8:00, Iowa 30.8 % (Eric then ruled 95, unmeasured).
   - (e) **How-to-Play:** light and heavy now read "Homes in on the loudest ship nearby." (was "Homes in more with each tier.").

## Story

As a captain,
I want more to choose from when I fill my four consumable slots — the Flash Shell back, and a buoy for radar, healing, shield or speed,
so that the four I carry are a real choice and a buoy does more than soak a torpedo.

**Eric's brief (2026-10-27, verbatim):** *"Right now there are six choices, of which the player picks four. Lets add back in the Flash Shell. Lets also split the Buoy into Four varieties... Radar, Healing, Shield, and Speed. All of them will have the same 50 HP stats and will soak up torpedoes/attract homing torpedoes as a preference over ships, but each of them has a different special area effect."* Ruled 2026-10-27 (epic-10 amendment 2): FLASH SHELLS returns as it was (epic-8 amendment 132) BESIDE the FOULING SHELL; the plain DECOY BUOY is GONE; "friendly ships" = the owner's own ship (no teams). Re-ruled 2026-10-28 at the dev invocation: **every buoy has a 750 u effect radius and expires 30 s after the drop (not 1:00)** — the RADAR BUOY's "1 minute" is likewise 30 s.

## Eric's rulings (2026-10-28, AskUserQuestion at the spec step — the amendment's text)

0. **(Standing, in the invocation.)** *"all buoys will have 750 unit range on their effect and will last for 30s (rather than 1 min)."* Supersedes amendment 2(c)/(d)'s 1:00.
1. **Dazzle leaves 1/4 of SIGHT range** (Eric: *"1/4 of sight range, which is i believe 1/12 of intel range"* — true today: sight 500 u / 4 = 125 u = radar 1500 u / 12). Pinned as a fraction of sight (`sightFraction 0.25 × sightRange`), the intel-range reading being his equivalence check. 10 s, one r150 u burst, never stacks (a second flash keeps the LATER expiry), radar untouched, DAZZLED tell — unchanged from amendment 132. Rejected: 1/8 of intel range (187.5 u today); 1/8 of sight range.
2. **FLASH SHELLS reaches 1000 u, like the FOULING SHELL** (`CONFIG.foulingShell.rangeU = RUNG*8`). The two click-aimed shells share one reach; aim preview and bot tactic reuse the fouling shell's. Rejected: the full radar rung (1500 u).
3. **Homing law: any ENEMY buoy inside the fish's acquire range wins.** If one or more enemy buoys are within `homingAcquireRange` (120 u), the fish locks the NEAREST BUOY and ignores every hull; otherwise the nearest hull as today. Own buoys are never a target (no friendly fire). Rejected: nearest wins with buoy on ties; a longer acquire range for buoys.
4. **SHIELD BUOY: no cap; the buoy shield is eaten before the SHIELD BLOCK.** The 30 s life bounds the pool at ≈30 % of max hp. Damage order: armor → buoy shield → SHIELD BLOCK's 100 hp → hull. Rejected: SHIELD BLOCK eaten first; a 25 % cap.
5. **Same-kind buoys do NOT stack: a kind counts once.** Inside any number of HEALING BUOYs you heal 2 %/s; one shield pool grows at 1 %/s; one +25 % speed. RADAR BUOYs each sweep their own spot (coverage adds — that is the point of placing two). Rejected: additive stacking.
6. **SPEED BUOY + the Speedboat's Shift add on the BASE: +50 % together.** Each adds a quarter of the rated top speed. Rejected: multiply (+56 %); no stacking.
7. **Bot appetite: neutral like every other line.** Flash fires like the fouling shell (engaged, live contact in sight); a buoy drops where the decoy did (torpedo inbound, or the radar-decoy want), with RADAR dropped when no contact is held, HEALING when hurt, SHIELD when engaged, SPEED when disengaging. No weight tuning this story. Rejected: eager buoys.
8. **Version 0.19.2** (cycle 208). Rejected: deciding at the PR.
9. **(Chat, same session, after the rounds.)** *"I would like all buoys to get a 360 degree firing arc so they can be placed anywhere around the ship. Placement range should not change."* — every buoy's arc is `'full'` (the decoy's rear sector is gone for buoys); the placement range stays `CONFIG.mine.placeRange`; the mines keep their rear sector.

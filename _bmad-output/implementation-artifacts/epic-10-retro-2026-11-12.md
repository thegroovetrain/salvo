## 4. Key Insights

1. **Hold the design sitting before the first story of an epic, not at the second.** The first-minute-objective sitting rewrote the epic and retired its first two stories. Epic 11 already does this: the bench page gates 11.1.
2. **A number is taken at PR time, never at story start.** With sessions running in parallel, an amendment / cycle / version number picked early is a guess; four stories paid for it.
3. **A candidate waiting on Eric's yes blocks the PR.** Both looks that shipped provisional were ruled differently or late; the nine that waited were not reworked for their look.
4. **Bots are where the defects are.** Six of thirteen gates found bot faults, including both highs. A story that changes a weapon or a hull changes the bots, and the gate should hunt there first.
5. **A status nobody stamps at the merge is never stamped.** Second retro running: eleven rows then, three now.
6. **How-to-Play written per epic would have been written twice.** Epic 11 moves the reaches again; one rewrite at 11.11 is the cheaper record — at the cost of a stale page until then.

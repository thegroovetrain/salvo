## Intent

**Problem:** The belt (slots 5–8) exists but nothing can enter it: a `stock` effect is a no-op in the shared fold, no `canStock` predicate exists, the digits `1`–`4` are refit-only (amendment 27), a belt click is silent (amendment 30), and the refit card is still the interim one-sentence face (amendment 8) at 216×236 with a 300 px prose tooltip. Stories 8.8, 8.10, 8.15 and 8.16 all hang off the belt's stock/fire path and the ratified five-row card.

**Approach:** Build the MECHANISM, not the content. One shared `canStock`/`stock` fold both sides run; a consumable stack `{ n: copiesHeld, reloadMsLeft: 0 }` with no reload; a used copy leaves `cards` so the client's replay and the server agree by construction; the two activation channels split by `isWeapon`; digits read the refit window's state at keydown with an inert grace after any close; a refused pick is a silent server no-op and the card is greyed BEFORE the press; the refit card becomes the ratified 216×226 five-row face from `hud-composite-3.html`; the slot tooltip keeps its width and gains "line + tier" / "consumable + stock". **All five consumable stubs STAY SET** (Eric, amendment 41): nothing new is drawable, the belt is empty on staging until 8.8, and `PROTOCOL_VERSION` stays 52.

## Design Notes

- **Why a used copy leaves `cards`:** the client rebuilds slot contents by replaying `cards`; if a use only decremented `n` server-side, the replay would restock on every respawn and disagree with `ammo` after a refresh. Removing the copy makes `cards` the single truth for both `equipmentId` and `n`, with no new wire field.
- **Why `SlotItemId` and not a wider `EquipmentId`:** five total records (`EquipmentRows`, stat-field whitelist, names, glyphs, weapon table) are keyed by `EquipmentId` and every consumable entry in them would be a lie (a stack has no stat row). A union at the ONE place that holds either — the slot — keeps every record honest and forces a narrowing guard at each read.
- **Why the registry ships empty:** Eric ruled the belt empty until 8.8; the factory + seam exist so 8.8 adds one row and one flag flip with no plumbing.
- **Why the clear-at-zero lives in the gate:** it is the only path to `activate()`, so "same tick" has one home and no row mutates its own slot.

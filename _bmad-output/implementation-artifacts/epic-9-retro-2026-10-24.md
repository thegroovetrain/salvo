## 4. Key Insights

1. **Pre-installing a module for a future epic is debt with a fuse.** 8.0's unmounted `@colyseus/auth`/`database` dragged an account decision into a dependency story and killed it. Install a dependency in the story that mounts it, never ahead.
2. **The loop is the process; size for it.** Two retros running, Eric's staging play produces the numbers. First-session cycles are 40 % of an epic's cycles; plans that assume one cycle per story are wrong by 2×.
3. **Render the real math live, then ask.** Fog (172) and the alerts page (181) were ruled in one sitting each because the page ran the shipped function. The disc build that preceded the fog page is the counter-example: a day of review-gated code replaced on sight.
4. **A contract test survives; a pin does not.** 9.14's rules (no wall-clock, no PV literal, no copy pin, no coincidence of two ruled numbers, derive-never-enumerate) are now in project-context.md; the suite shrank by 124 net tests and lost nothing it guarded.
5. **A scripted scenario is a test; a heuristic pilot is weather.** The two smokes went from "ledgered flake" to first-try the day they stopped sailing bots (214). The other five heuristic-pilot smokes are still weather.
6. **Flags, pointers and statuses nobody acts on rot.** The follow-up flag retired as a category (215); 446 ledger entries re-stamped; and this retro found eleven story rows still at `review` after their PRs merged. Status that is not stamped at the PR is stamped never.

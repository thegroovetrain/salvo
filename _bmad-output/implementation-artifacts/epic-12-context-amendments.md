## Create-story 2026-11-19 — Story 12.4 sitting: XP airdrops, the level stat roll, wreck crates, supply drops replace radio beacons (no build cycle; `12-4-airdrops-and-radio-beacons.md`)

5. **EVERY AIRDROP IS AN XP CRATE; EVERY LEVEL ROLLS A DIE PER STAT; A WRECK PAYS +1 RANDOM STAT PER LEVEL THE VICTIM HAD; RADIO BEACONS ARE REMOVED AND THEIR SPAWNS BECOME SUPPLY DROPS OF 10 XP CRATES.** Eric 2026-11-19 (`/gds-create-story 12-4` args + two AskUserQuestion rounds). Never re-ask (a)–(h).
   - (a) **XP crates:** *"I want to turn all airdrops into experience pickups. Lets say they grant 10% experience. The icon should be a square with "+XP" inside it."* A crate grants 0.1 of a level through the ordinary XP pipeline (`grantXp`). Color: *"XP gauge color"* (phosphor today; whatever the HUD's XP bar wears if 12.3 recolors it). The minute schedule (2 per minute remaining to 12:00), the 15 u pickup disc and the sight / radar rules are unchanged.
   - (b) **The level stat roll:** *"every time I level I want to gain 0, 1, 2, or 3 points per stat, randomly. Treat it like a dice roll, where the faces are 0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 3. (If there are no more upgrade cards in my deck, I should continue to gain this stat roll)."* Each completed level rolls the die once per index stat (ARMOR, POWER, ENGINES, RELOAD, INTEL) onto the hull's index bonus. It is independent of the card offer.
   - (c) **The out-of-cards +1 is retired:** *"Roll replaces it"*. A hull with nothing dealable banks no level and gets no extra point; the roll is its growth.
   - (d) **The amber flash:** *"When I gain a level, the increased stat's number in the HUD stat display should turn amber for a moment and then fade back to its normal color to indicate what has changed."*
   - (e) **Wreck crates:** *"Wreck crate is a different concept altogether … A wreck is worth +1 point to a random stat for each level that ship had gained before being sunk. So if I sink someone at level 5, it provides +1 to a random stat five times."* It keeps the white salvage icon; a ship sunk at level 0 leaves NO wreck (*"Keep salvage icon; lvl 0 = none"*). Its spawn rules (participants only, a scuttle only in combat, never in respawn mode) are unchanged.
   - (f) **Radio beacons are removed; supply drops replace them:** *"Beacons are being removed altogether. Instead, treat them as a supply drop of XP crates, say 10 of them in a bunch. Same spawn characteristics, and I want their locations marked on the map before they drop."* Same placement and timing as the beacons (counts 7 / 5 / 3 per ring cycle, charted at the cycle start, landing at `surfaceMs` 1:00 later, edge margin, captain keep-out, min spacing). The 10 crates scatter at random inside the old 500 u circle without overlapping (*"Inside the old 500 u circle"*). The crates are ordinary XP crates (a). No supply drop has hp, armor, a body, a pot or a kill payout.
   - (g) **The map mark:** *"It disappears when the crates land, it should fade out over 10 sec or something."* Public to every player from charting (the schema seat the beacon chart used); fades out over ~10 s from the landing moment.
   - (h) **Bots:** *"Head for charted drops"* — a bot may steer to a charted supply drop as it went to beacons, then collect the crates there. The bot port's chart widening (epic-10 amendment 6(g)) carries over to the supply-drop chart.
   - **Readings of record, for Eric's veto (not asked):** the roll fires only on a completed level (`level` +1), never on the countdown's opening hand; a sinking or sunk hull's levels roll nothing (amendment 13 R7's "once sinking, you're done"); the wreck's +1s land uniformly on the five index stats; the amber flash fires on ANY rise of a stat's bonus (level roll or wreck); an XP crate's pickup plays the chime with no toast (no copy unasked), a wreck keeps its stat toast; the minute crates lose their beacon keep-out; the beacon-surfaced sound plays when a supply drop lands; the How-to-Play beacon paragraph is replaced by a draft Eric rewrites.

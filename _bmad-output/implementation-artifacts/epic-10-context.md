## Technical Decisions

- Index model: 1000 HP per hull, shown only as a whole-number percent; ARMOR, POWER, RELOAD, ENGINES, INTEL and RANGE are indices folded once in `effectiveStats()`. RUDDER is removed — every captain hull turns at the baseline. Equal broadside damage across hulls; POWER replaces armor piercing in the damage gate; ramming mass = ARMOR. Ship cards: ARMOR / POWER / INTEL +20, RELOAD / ENGINES / RANGE +10. A maxed hull's further levels each grant +1 to a random index stat.
- Battleships: Yamato, Bismarck, HMS Warspite, USS Iowa, machine-traced and true to scale; the silhouette is the hitbox. Turret layouts 3×3 or 4×2, each turret with its own traverse (90°/s, shared sim, hard stop at the train limit) and 10 s reload. A click considers only ready turrets whose arc contains the aim: an on-target one fires, else the closest-bearing one fires early along its current bearing; a turret whose arc the cursor has left holds its angle. Secondaries run on heat, fire on right mouse at half the main reach.
- Ship rendering: all linework is the relation color (cyan own/allied, red enemy) on a per-hull skin; the 20-hue wheel is gone. The Shift is the only seat pick (default Bismarck + Speed Boost).
- Equipment: three family slots — `Q` torpedo, `E` mine, `R` special shell — empty at spawn, filled only by cards, one kind per slot per match; readying a slot makes the next left click fire it. HULL REPAIR (`C`) and SMOKE (`V`) are per-ship cooldown abilities, untouched by RELOAD. Passive regen is back (2 % of missing hp per second after 15 s without hull damage). RELOAD scales the main battery and the Q/E/R weapons only (each weapon tier adds +10 to that weapon's own reload score). Torpedoes fire from training deck launchers into per-ship side bands; mines drop from stern racks.
- Offers: Ship / Equipment / wildcard / wildcard; a wildcard is a 50/50 coin; a redraw re-rolls everything.
- Beacons: 8 / 4 / 2 per ring cycle, none at sudden death; charted globally from the cycle's start, surface at 1:00, float one minute paying 3 levels/min split among hulls in range, then sink over a minute; solid and shootable (1000 HP, ARMOR 100); destroying one sinks it early and pays 2 levels split like a kill. Airdrops: random permanent index bonuses, sail-over pickup, painted on radar by imprint.
- Anti-cheat: the six perception exceptions are extended, never widened; new contact kinds/events extend the invariant tests. Wire or client-read catalog changes bump `PROTOCOL_VERSION`.

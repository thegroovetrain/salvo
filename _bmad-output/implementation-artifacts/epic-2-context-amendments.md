## 2026-07-24 — Eric rulings, Story 2.1 invocation (bmad-dev-auto 2-1)

Source: Eric, verbatim invocation arguments to the Story 2.1 dev-auto run.

1. **Refit window binding is TAB, a toggle — supersedes SPACE-hold.** TAB opens the upgrade/refit dialog; pressing TAB again closes it without choosing. This replaces the ratified "SPACE is hold, never toggle — absolute" design (epics.md Story 2.1 AC, EXPERIENCE.md refit flow) AND the interregnum bare-CTRL toggle. Eric: "Tab should open the upgrade dialog rather than space."
2. **In-modal picks: number keys 1–4 select a card; clicking a card also selects.** A click-pick selects the upgrade and closes the modal and must NOT fire the gun. Spending closes the modal.
3. **The ~150–200 ms post-release number-key grace window is superseded.** It existed to disambiguate keydowns around SPACE release. Under the TAB toggle, a number key's meaning is evaluated against the modal's open state at its own keydown; outside the modal, number keys are refit-or-nothing (they do nothing — the old digit slot-priming and the closed-window spend behavior are both deleted).
4. **Q E R F confirmed as the scheme; F is reserved specifically for the Foghorn.** Q/E = the two class-special slots, R = the pickup/extra slot (inert while empty), F = reserved, not a slot key — and its future occupant is the Foghorn (pre-rules Story 4.5's "binds the key with Eric"). Example given: Torpedo Boat Q = torpedo, E = speed boost, R = empty.
5. **Gun is default and always selected; weapon primes revert to gun.** Once a primed weapon (e.g. torpedo) is fired it automatically switches back to the gun; pressing its key again without firing also switches back to the gun.

## 2026-08-04 — Eric rulings, DAMAGE CONTROL heal strip (bmad-dev-auto interstitial cycle 46)

Source: Eric, direct answers to the seven surfaced questions at the pre-implementation question gate (AskUserQuestion, this run).

58. **Healing returns as an always-available strip, NOT a card.** `CONFIG.offer.size` stays 4 and the ratified UX-DR14 four-card row (216px cards, 20px gaps, 924px) is untouched; the heal is a separate permanent element BELOW the row. This SUPERSEDES amendment 7's "REPAIR is removed" for the strip only (the interregnum CTRL+E repair row stays dead — this is a new surface, not its restoration) and DISCHARGES amendment 38's "Eric's 10%-over-5s level-heal FILED TO THE FUTURE TTK PASS" — this cycle IS that pass. Explicitly REJECTED: dropping the draw to 3 cards so heal could be the 4th (would have superseded amendment 38's four-different-lines law and thinned every level's choice), and a 5th card (1160px row, 60px margin at the 1280x614 logical floor, supersedes UX-DR14).
59. **Flat 25 instant + 25 pooled on EVERY hull.** No maxHp scaling, no class variation, no upgrade scaling. 50 hp total is 40% of a Torpedo Boat (125), 33% of a Mine Layer (150), 29% of a Battleship (175) — the heal is deliberately a proportionally stronger sustain tool on light hulls. This REPLACES the percentage framing recorded in amendment 38 ("10%-over-5s"). Explicitly REJECTED: percentage-of-maxHp, and deriving from base hull hp to resist `shipHull` cap growth.
60. **Pools ADD; the rate NEVER changes.** A second heal spent mid-drain adds 25 to the remaining pool and it runs longer — never faster. Two heals = 10s at 5 hp/s, never 5s at 10 hp/s. Rationale of record: refresh semantics (discard the remainder) teach timer-babysitting, the failure mode Blizzard patched out of WoW with the pandemic rule; rate-stacking (the Path of Exile flask model) has no check here because — unlike EVERY battle-royale heal — this one has no channel to interrupt, so three levels dumped at once would be 75 instant plus 15 hp/s. Pool-adds is timing-neutral: a level is always worth exactly 50 hp, so the only question is "do I need HP now". It also matches the house DoT precedent, where overlapping burns stack per distinct OWNER and never per application from one source (`dotKey(ownerId, victimId)`).
61. **The pool drains on the WALL CLOCK; overflow is LOST.** Healing at 174/175 wastes almost the whole level. Explicitly REJECTED: pausing the pool at full HP and paying it out on the next damage (that is mechanically a SHIELD — a full-HP player could pre-load before a fight), and a hybrid pause-with-expiry.
